# Open world: Earth and the sky

The world is built in four layers:

| Layer | What it is | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| Sky | The whole planet seen from above. You fly across it fast and come down anywhere. | `scenes/world/sky.tscn` (`SkyWorld`) |
| Earth | One continuous ground map: sea, coasts, plains, forests, wastes, mountains, snow, rivers and paths. | `scenes/world/earth.tscn` (`OpenWorld` + `EarthTerrain`) |
| Regions | Hand-made places set into Earth: West City, the Rocky Wastes and Kame Island. | `kame_island.tscn`, `west_city_outskirts.tscn`, `rocky_wastes.tscn` |
| Interiors | Rooms behind doors: Kame House and the Capsule Corp training chamber. | `kame_house_interior.tscn`, `capsule_training_chamber.tscn` |

## The planet

The geography is our own design. The reference maps gave the structure:
a large continent with a mountain wall to the north, islands spread across
the sea, and different biomes for each stage of the journey.

### How it plays (design)

- **Kame Island (start):** on its own in the eastern sea. It is the
  tutorial, with Roshi, the sparring ring and the house.
- **The way out:** the capsule ship to West City, or flying once you can.
  The sea keeps beginners on the island without an invisible wall.
- **West City and Capsule Corp:** the hub, with shops, Bulma, the
  training chamber and the study spots.
- **Rocky Wastes:** right next to the city, the first fighting ground. The
  city road fades into a dirt trail.
- **Paths:** they lead from the hub to the next goals: north to Ginger
  Town, east to East City and Mount Paozu, south-east to the Cell Games
  arena. Rivers can only be crossed on foot at their bridges.
- **Northern Mountains:** a rock wall you cannot walk through, only fly
  over. The Snowy Highlands sit behind it.
- **Holy Land Forest (Korin Tower), the Southern Continent and the
  Tropical Islands:** across the sea, for when you fly well.
- **Landmarks without content yet:** Ginger Town, East City, Mount Paozu,
  Cell Games, Korin Tower and the Snowy Highlands are already on the map,
  in the sky and in the zone names. They are ready for their own regions.

## Ground (Earth)

- **Scale:** 208 x 144 tiles of 32 px (6656 x 4608 px). Walking across the
  main continent takes about a minute; fast flight takes about half that.
- **Layout:** `assets/world/earth_layout.png` holds one pixel per tile, and
  its colour is the biome. It is generated by `tools/world/build_earth_layout.py`,
  together with the sky image, the clouds and `world/earth_layout_data.gd`
  (zones, landmarks, region placement).
- **Terrain:** `world/earth_terrain.gd` paints it with HU2 turfs:
  - water: `NewTurfs` Water/OWater;
  - beach: the Kame Island sand;
  - plains and forest: Grass and DarkGrass;
  - wastes: the wastes ground;
  - mountains: `HighwayCliffs` rock;
  - snow: Snow;
  - paths: `HighwayCliffs` Path;
  - bridges: drawn in code.
- **Decoration:** forests get trees, the plains get a few, and beaches near
  water get palm trees. Trees are solid at the trunk.
- **Collision:** sea, rivers and mountains block walkers. Flyers pass over
  everything, but only land on walkable ground.
- **Zones:** when you walk or fly into a zone, the location banner names it
  (NORTHERN MOUNTAINS, HOLY LAND FOREST...). Regions keep their own banners.
- **Depth:** the world is taller than Godot's z range (+-4096), so
  `WorldDepth` measures depth from an origin that follows the player in
  512 px steps. Near the player the order stays pixel exact.

## Sky

- **Going up:** while flying over Earth, press U. The sky shows the whole
  planet at 6 px per tile, so 1 px flown in the sky covers 5.33 px of
  ground. Interiors cannot go up.
- **Moving:** it uses normal flight: Space for fast flight, with no ki cost.
  There is no fighting up here.
- **Clouds and names:** clouds drift by and thin out around you. Every
  landmark has a marker and its name. The top of the screen shows what is
  below.
- **Coming down:** press U or V. You arrive on Earth exactly below, already
  descending. Over water or mountains you land on the nearest ground.

## Adding a region

1. **Make the scene.** Give it a `CameraBounds` (`WorldCameraBounds`) with
   its size. Islands add `is_landable_point(point)` and draw their own coast.
2. **Reserve its place.** Add it to `REGIONS` in
   `tools/world/build_earth_layout.py` and run the script.
3. **Place it.** Instance it in `earth.tscn` at that tile x 32. List any
   walls it no longer needs in `removed_nodes`.
